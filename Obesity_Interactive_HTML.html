Notebook Workflow

The process moves from data understanding to model selection, sensitivity checks, and deployment preparation.

Why the classes were grouped

The original target has 7 weight-related labels. For this notebook, they were grouped into 4 clinically clearer classes: Underweight, Normal, Overweight, and Obesity.

This makes the model more aligned with practical screening. Once a case falls into obesity, the realistic action is clinical assessment or referral rather than separating fine obesity subtypes in the prediction step.

BMI as a Label-Proxy

This sensitivity check compares BMI-only, without-BMI, and the selected full model using the same selected model type.

Interpretation

BMI alone reaches Macro F1 = 0.8955, close to the full model's validation Macro F1. Removing BMI reduces Macro F1 to 0.7367, a drop of 0.1772.

This does not mean test-set leakage. It means BMI carries a strong target-definition signal because obesity classes are strongly linked to BMI-defined weight categories.
